ODETTE PRODUCTION FACTORY

Privacy notice

27 November 2026

Odette Production Factory is a private editorial production workflow intended for its owner, not a public service or a general-purpose Google Drive application.

Purpose and Google data

The planned Google Drive integration requests authorization to retrieve production source files, such as artwork, scripts, audio and manifests, and to store rendered media, quality-control records and production checkpoints. Depending on the Google permissions granted, the authorization may technically permit access to additional Drive files. The workflow is intended to restrict processing to designated production files and folders.

Processing and disclosure

Authorized production files may be processed by private GitHub Actions workflows and stored in Google Drive. Approved media may be transferred to Metricool for publication only after the owner authorizes release. Google user data is not intended to be sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Access is limited to the production purpose. The integration is under development; this notice describes its intended operation and does not assert that all controls have been deployed.

Security and retention

OAuth credentials must be stored in protected secret storage, never in public source code or logs. Temporary processing files and workflow artifacts are intended to be removed when no longer needed; master files and records remain in the owner's Drive until the owner removes them. The owner can revoke Google access at any time via Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized requests but does not itself delete previously exported copies.

Google API policy

The intended use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
