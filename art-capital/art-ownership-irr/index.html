Research hypothesis

How does the observed market base respond to changes in demand, friction, liquidity, inflation, currency and holding period?

This is a sensitivity engine, not a price forecast. Market turnover does not measure returns of individual artworks. Artwork-level repeat-sales, unsold lots, tax and provenance are not included.

Methodology

Multiplicative sensitivity of 2025 aggregate market sales. Nominal proxy = base × (1+demand shock) × (1−transaction friction) × (1−liquidity haircut) × (1+FX change). Real proxy = nominal / (1+inflation)^holding period. The scenarios do not represent observed changes or a validated econometric model.
