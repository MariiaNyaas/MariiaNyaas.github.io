01 / THE MARKET DISLOCATION

Established art hubs are not the entire addressable market.

Global art sales are concentrated in a handful of countries. But international travel, rising income, digital connectivity and institutional stability do not necessarily move together. Their divergence may identify places worth testing before competitors commit to permanent space.

This study measures conditions that may support customer acquisition, not verified art expenditure. No country ranking should be read as a forecast of artwork demand.

04 / HUMAN INTERPRETATION

What does this mean for an art business?

A high proxy score is a reason to investigate local collectors, galleries and cultural institutions — not a reason to ship inventory blindly.

Suggested actions are research hypotheses. Before committing capital, validate local art transaction evidence, gallery competition, collector interviews, import/export rules, shipping, insurance and provenance requirements.

06 / DATA DICTIONARY & MODEL AUDIT

Fourteen signals. One reproducible decision framework.

Method: fetch World Bank API series individually; preserve every numeric country-year observation and its year. For the cross-section, take the most recent non-missing 2020–2024 observation per country and indicator. Winsorize each variable to its observed 5th–95th percentile across the sample, then min-max normalize; logarithmically transform strongly skewed level variables. Reverse inflation volatility, air pollution and political instability only as explicitly indicated in the dictionary. Weight observed indicators within four pillars and redistribute pillar weights across available pillars; require at least eight observed indicators. The sample-relative composite does not imply causation. There is no statistical training or backtest; use it for exploratory screening only. Missing data are not imputed.

Limitations: GDP per capita is not collector wealth; tourism is not cultural tourism; tertiary enrolment is not cultural participation; political stability is a governance estimate, not an assessment of any government; PM2.5 is an environmental proxy, not a direct artwork conservation cost. National data hide city-level differences. Currency, tariffs, customs, shipping, gallery rents, artist supply, museum density, collector transactions, fair calendars and climate hazard exposure require city-specific due diligence. Data publication lags and methodological revisions vary.
