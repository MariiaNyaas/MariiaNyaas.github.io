Before the Hammer Falls

Speculative trading, liquidity illusion and early-warning signals in art markets. A multi-country evidence explorer and six scenario laboratories for collectors, auction houses, dealers and wealth managers.

Evidence boundary: this is a research prototype, not a validated bubble predictor. Country comparisons use 2025 aggregate published market data; country-level indicators are kept separate from any historical auction series until comparable observations are verified. The two datasets are not interchangeable. No transaction-level global big-data feed is claimed.

Commercial decision layer / conditional economics

Translate market stress into a business decision

Use a hypothetical holding to quantify exit proceeds and financing risk. These outputs are mechanical sensitivities; they are not empirical price forecasts.

Estimated net sale proceeds
—

Appraised value × (1 − exit discount) × (1 − seller fee)

Net equity after debt
—

Net sale proceeds less debt; negative values indicate a shortfall.

Illustrative collateral shortfall
—

Debt × minimum coverage − stressed collateral value, floored at zero.

05 / Research protocol

What would qualify as genuine bubble early warning?

Define a future adverse outcome before looking at the data, such as a ≥25% repeat-sale price drawdown over the next 24 months combined with a sell-through contraction. Use transaction-level estimates and unsold lots; distinguish price inflation from composition shifts, provenance, artist career stage, inflation and FX. Estimate signals on rolling windows using only information available at each decision date. Compare a baseline against multivariate hazard/logistic and right-tail explosiveness diagnostics (e.g., GSADF), with nested time-series backtesting, confidence intervals, precision/recall, false alarms, calibration and survivorship checks.

Critical limitation: without harmonised artwork-level transaction histories, repeat-sale IDs, unsold lots and timestamps across markets, it would be scientifically indefensible to claim that the present dashboard detects future art bubbles. The country explorer is a 2025 cross-sectional exposure map; the six labs are conditional scenarios.
