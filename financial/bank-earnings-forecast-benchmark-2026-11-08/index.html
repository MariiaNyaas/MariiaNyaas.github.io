Bank Earnings: When More Accounting Drivers Fail the Forecast Test

Norwegian bank income statements offer an unusually revealing methodological trap: quarterly accounts are year-to-date cumulative. A forecast comparison that ignores this fact confuses accounting accumulation with economic persistence.

Research Intelligence Brief

The hypothesis, tested rather than assumed

Finding: Lagged interest income and expense do not improve forecast accuracy over simple benchmarks in the tested specification. These are retrospective pseudo-out-of-sample estimates, not demonstrated real-time forecasting performance.

Pattern: external accounting drivers beat autoregressive controls across independent, vintage-consistent periods → investigate operational adoption. Alternative explanations: accounting identities, changing balance-sheet mix, structural breaks, missing deposit rates, reporting revisions, and insufficient training observations. Needed evidence: real-time vintages, alternative penalties and lag structures, uncertainty bands, and externally held-out samples.

What would change our mind? Stable, economically meaningful reductions in forecast error on untouched first-release data after controlling for structural shifts.

Practical implication: use a transparent baseline before assigning governance weight to added financial ratios or drivers.

Reproducible method and limitations

Source: SSB 08113 — Financial corporations, quarterly income statement, user export dated 8 November 2026. Columns: 02 Banker, 2009 Q2–2026 Q3. Year-to-date values converted to quarterly flows by subtracting the previous quarter within each calendar year; Q1 is unchanged. The initial 2009 Q2 observation is cumulative and must not be treated as a true standalone quarter; the lagged supervised evaluation begins later.

Targets: quarterly net interest income and pre-tax profit, NOK million. Baselines: previous-quarter value and Ridge autoregression with 1-, 2- and 4-quarter lags. Extended Ridge: the same autoregressive terms plus one-quarter-lagged interest income and expense for NII; lagged NII and interest expense for pre-tax profit. Fixed ridge penalty α=1000 in raw NOK-million feature units. Training expands each quarter; 28 initial complete training observations, followed by 38 held-out sequential predictions. RMSE and MAE are calculated on the same 38 targets.

Important: Ridge α=1000 is not hyperparameter-tuned or standardized; this is a diagnostic comparison, not a validated optimal forecasting model. Data are revised current-vintage exports, not historic first-release vintages. Release-date availability, structural changes, and uncertainty of loss differences have not been established. Some income statement definitions may have changed during the sample. There is no statistical significance claim, no causal inference and no investment recommendation.
