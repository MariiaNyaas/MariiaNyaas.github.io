# The Electricity–Credit Nexus

The stable Oslo all-industry series contains 226 monthly observations from January 2008 to October 2026; counts are not default rates.

Across 36 expanding-origin forecasts, the log-AR/seasonal model has MAE 11.80, versus 15.47 for the previous-year month: a 23.7% reduction.

In-sample R² is only 0.079; a useful benchmark forecast does not validate the proposed electricity transmission mechanism.

The supplied Eurostat electricity export covers 2021-S2 to 2026-S1. Ten half-years cannot support a credible multi-lag credit model with structural breaks.
