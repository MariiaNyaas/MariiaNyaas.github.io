# Who Captures the AI Energy Dividend?

Consolidated general-government revenue in 2024 is NOK 3203.407 billion; expenditure is 2524.615 billion and net lending is 678.792 billion.

Aggregate 2024 dividends are NOK 403.313 billion. This national-accounts category is not an estimate of AI or electricity-sector dividends.

The 2025 electricity balance records 160,454 GWh primary electricity production, 11,515 GWh imports and 34,315 GWh exports. Net exports are 22,800 GWh.

A hypothetical 100 MW load at 90% utilisation consumes 788.4 GWh annually. Allocation of its fiscal surplus requires assumptions about prices, costs, ownership and public support.
