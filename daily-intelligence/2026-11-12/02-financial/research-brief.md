# The Economics of Stored Electricity

At 90% efficiency, NO1’s frozen schedule earns 151.79 NOK per 1 MWh daily charge in the 184-block holdout, before all operating and capital costs.

The same holdout yields a 475.86 NOK/day perfect-foresight upper bound. The frozen rule captures 31.9% of that gross opportunity.

A 2,000-draw circular seven-day block bootstrap puts the NO1 mean between 69.68 and 238.41 NOK/day at the 95% level. This is sampling uncertainty, not a future-return interval.

At 80% efficiency the NO1 fixed-rule mean falls to 66.48 NOK/day. Excluding the final source block changes the 90% result to 153.01.
