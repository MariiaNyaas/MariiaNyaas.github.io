# The Depreciation Illusion

Oracle’s fiscal year ended 2026-05-31: operating cash flow of 31.977 billion USD and cash PP&E investment of 55.663 billion imply a -23.686 billion cash-flow residual before other investing/financing items.

Microsoft’s fiscal year ended June 2026 and the December-2025 reporters are not a common calendar-year panel; the dashboard preserves fiscal dates.

Amazon and NVIDIA use a productive-assets cash investment tag in recent filings. Tag migrations are resolved by period, never by taking an obsolete tag as current.

A hypothetical 100 billion USD cohort depreciated over six rather than three years changes annual pre-tax expense by 16.667 billion USD at zero salvage. This is arithmetic, not evidence of misstatement.
