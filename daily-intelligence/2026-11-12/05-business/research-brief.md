# Europe’s AI Adoption Paradox

EU adoption among enterprises with at least ten employees rises from 8.06% in 2023 to 19.95% in 2025, a gain of 11.89 percentage points.

For 2025, adoption is 17.00% among small enterprises, 30.36% among medium enterprises and 55.03% among large enterprises in the supplied Eurostat export.

Across 27 EU countries, a one-point higher 2024 adoption share is associated with -0.0171 percentage points of 2025 productivity-per-hour growth; the HC3 95% interval is [-0.0845, 0.0503].

A model trained on 2023 adoption and 2024 growth has 2025 holdout RMSE 1.619 pp, against 1.624 for a training-period mean forecast. This offers little predictive discrimination.
