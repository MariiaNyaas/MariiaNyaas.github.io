A stewardship triage map of voting-rights wedges

Research Intelligence Brief

  1. The 30 June 2026 equity snapshot contains 7,077 rows with both reported ownership and voting percentages.
  2. At an absolute 0.10-percentage-point threshold, 414 rows enter the review set, representing 13.13% of listed equity market value.
  3. Raising that threshold to 0.50 percentage points reduces the review set to 202 rows; at 1.00 percentage point it is 63 rows.
  4. The incorporation-country field is empty in all 7,077 rows. Legal-domicile analysis is therefore blocked rather than imputed.

The mechanism and the test

Economic participation and voting influence are separate governance dimensions. A reported wedge is a triage signal for checking share classes, voting arrangements and data definitions. Neither its sign nor its size alone establishes entrenchment, control or a return penalty. The new research question concerns stewardship workflow rather than portfolio concentration.

Methodology & limitations

Read equity holdings in UTF-16, semicolon separated. Parse reported percent strings; retain missing values. Wedge = voting percentage − ownership percentage, rounded to the source’s two decimal places before threshold comparisons, in percentage points, not relative percent. Materiality uses absolute wedge ≥ threshold. Value share uses all exported listed-equity NOK values as denominator; it is not the total sovereign fund. Row counts are holdings rows, not asserted unique ultimate issuers. Exact reported rounded values are not adjusted for unobserved precision. Sensitivity thresholds 0.05/0.10/0.25/0.50/1.00 pp. Laboratory ranks eligible rows by market value, applies a user-chosen proportional price shock and limits review capacity. A uniform price shock changes value, not the reported voting percentage.

Snapshot 30 June 2026. Rounded percentages; 0.1 percentage-point threshold is a sensitivity assumption. Differences may reflect share classes or reporting structure; issuer verification needed before attributing governance causes. No causal return or control inference. Rounding can move boundary cases across thresholds. No cross-sectional return test is warranted without returns, share-class reconciliation and confounders. Incorporation data is NOT VERIFIED / unavailable and excluded from the research claim.
