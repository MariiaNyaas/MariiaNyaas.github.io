Methodology / Public Finance Intelligence

The Revenue–Spending Asymmetry

SSB 10721, consolidated General government only, NOK million, 1995–2024. Do not add central and local totals. Adjacent growth = value_t/value_(t−1)−1. Identify contraction strictly at negative nominal revenue growth and inspect concurrent nominal expenditure growth. Reconcile A−E−F within one source unit. The Spearman rank statistic is exploratory and not a causal response estimate. Annual sample is short and regime dependent; no fiscal multiplier, sovereign default model or structural forecast is estimated. Laboratory anchors to a selected observed year, compounds an assumed annual revenue decline and spending increase, and reports nominal net lending in NOK billion.

Limits of identification

Nominal general-government flows through 2024; petroleum exposure, inflation and policy confound associations. Not the structural non-oil budget balance or fiscal-rule headroom; no sovereign PD inference. Latest observation is 2024, not 2026. No claim about today’s structural non-oil budget, fiscal-rule compliance, available cash or fiscal space. No GDP deflator, output gap or commodity-price instrument is supplied; real or causal adjustment estimates are NOT VERIFIED.
