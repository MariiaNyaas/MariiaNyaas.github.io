When Negative Power Prices Persist

Read the Strømpris worksheet; preserve all 8,760 rows, including the final all-zone zero. Multiply øre/kWh by 10 for NOK/MWh. Define a negative state strictly as price < 0. First 4,344 observations train the unconditional rate and two transition probabilities; remaining 4,416 observations evaluate one-step probabilities with the observed preceding state. No test refitting. Brier = mean squared probability error, compared with the training-only frequency. Bootstrap paired improvement with 2,000 circular draws and 24/168-observation blocks. Episode lengths are full-sample descriptive results. Laboratory: a two-state Markov expectation from a non-negative starting state, with user-selected probabilities; not a calibrated future-price forecast.

Limits of identification

Indexed 8760 observations; calendar and DST not authenticated. State persistence is not an executable trading strategy. Terminal zero row retained. Brier scores, not investment returns. No trading backtest, revenue stacking or negative-price investment recommendation. Conditional probabilities and clock-hour labels require timestamp authentication before operational use.
