The Cash Funding Frontier

Reported operating cash flow versus cash capital expenditure

EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE · EXPLICIT LIMITATIONS · USER SCENARIOS

Research Intelligence Brief

  1. Latest available full-year cash capex / operating cash flow is 174.07% for Oracle and 94.48% for Amazon.
  2. The same ratio is 63.38% for Microsoft, 60.18% for Meta, 55.52% for Alphabet and 5.88% for NVIDIA.
  3. These observations have different fiscal year ends: December 2025 through June 2026. They are not one synchronised calendar-year portfolio.
  4. Cash capex coverage measures a funding relationship; it does not measure AI investment alone or prove financial distress.

The mechanism and the test

Operating cash generation can fund physical investment without external capital only to the extent that cash remains after other claims. A ratio above 100% identifies a cash-capex funding gap within the reporting perimeter. It says nothing by itself about financing access, cash reserves, debt maturities or future investment returns.

Methodology & limitations

Six SEC Company Facts exports, CIKs 789019/1652044/1018724/1326801/1045810/1341439. USD, form 10-K, filing date ≤ 13 November 2026, annual duration 330–380 days, period ends from 2015. Resolve each period using latest filing, then documented tag priority; never sum alternative tags. CFO: NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities. Cash capex: PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment, falling back to PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets. Retain tag, accession, start/end and filing date for every selected record. Latest-vintage restatements are retained; no real-time backtest is claimed. CFO minus cash capex is a research metric, not a standardised free-cash-flow definition. Scenario: CFO×(1+CFO change) − capex×(1+capex change) − extra cash commitments.

Cash capex excludes some leases/noncash additions; CFO includes working-capital swings. Fiscal years differ; do not sum latest rows into a calendar-year total. Not an AI-specific spending measure. Missing payout tags remain missing. No empirical causal model or return prediction: the question is accounting reconciliation, so forecast backtesting is not applicable. Source-tag semantic completeness remains a limitation; no claim that cash capex covers total economic investment.

What Would Change Our Mind

  • A filing-note reconciliation showing material omitted lease or financing flows.
  • Persistently improved cash generation through the next investment cycle.
  • Evidence that the ratio is driven mainly by temporary working-capital effects.
