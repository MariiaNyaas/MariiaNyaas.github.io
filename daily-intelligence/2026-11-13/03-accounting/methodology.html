The Cash Funding Frontier

Six SEC Company Facts exports, CIKs 789019/1652044/1018724/1326801/1045810/1341439. USD, form 10-K, filing date ≤ 13 November 2026, annual duration 330–380 days, period ends from 2015. Resolve each period using latest filing, then documented tag priority; never sum alternative tags. CFO: NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities. Cash capex: PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment, falling back to PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets. Retain tag, accession, start/end and filing date for every selected record. Latest-vintage restatements are retained; no real-time backtest is claimed. CFO minus cash capex is a research metric, not a standardised free-cash-flow definition. Scenario: CFO×(1+CFO change) − capex×(1+capex change) − extra cash commitments.

Limits of identification

Cash capex excludes some leases/noncash additions; CFO includes working-capital swings. Fiscal years differ; do not sum latest rows into a calendar-year total. Not an AI-specific spending measure. Missing payout tags remain missing. No empirical causal model or return prediction: the question is accounting reconciliation, so forecast backtesting is not applicable. Source-tag semantic completeness remains a limitation; no claim that cash capex covers total economic investment.

Audit scope

Separate calculation paths re-read source records. This is automated review by the same AI session, not external human peer review. Publication checks are itemised in the audit.
