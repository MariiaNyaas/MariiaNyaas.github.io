Methodology / 22 November 2026

The Voice-to-Capital Mismatch

Research question

Where reported voting rights and economic ownership cease to be interchangeable

Data and calculation

NBIM listed-equity holdings report dated 30 June 2026, UTF-16 semicolon-separated CSV. Retain rows with numeric market value, voting percentage and ownership percentage. Gap = voting − ownership; report absolute gap, value-weighted absolute gap and threshold sensitivity at 0.10/0.25/0.50/1.00/2.00 pp. Denominator is market value of retained rows. Snapshot analysis; forecast backtesting is not applicable.

Identification boundary

One 30 June 2026 holdings snapshot. Reported voting and ownership percentages are not legal opinions, expected influence, engagement intensity or realised governance outcomes. Share classes, lending, local rules and meeting-specific eligibility may matter; no time-series persistence test is possible from this extract.

Validation classification

Temporal holdouts use only earlier rows at each origin but rely on the latest published vintage unless explicitly stated. The business test is leave-one-country-out cross-sectional validation, not a future-year temporal backtest. Snapshot governance analysis is not presented as forecast validation. User scenarios are deterministic assumptions, not empirical forecasts.
