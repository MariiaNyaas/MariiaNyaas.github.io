The Digital-Readiness Conversion Test

Research question

Does basic digital intensity improve a next-year AI-adoption baseline?

Data and calculation

Eurostat dissemination datasets isoc_e_dii and isoc_eb_ain2. Ten countries: Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Finland, Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Ireland and Poland. Retain enterprises with 10+ persons, all covered non-financial activities, percentage of enterprises. DII version 4 at least-basic level in 2024; AI use in 2024 and 2025. For each country, fit on the other nine and predict its 2025 AI use from 2024 DII and 2024 AI use. Compare with 2024 AI-use persistence. Country resampling, 5,000 draws.

Identification boundary

Ten-country leave-one-country-out validation, using 2024 DII version 4 and 2024 AI use to estimate 2025 AI use. This is cross-country validation, not a new-year temporal backtest. Repeated enterprise surveys are not firm-level transitions, and supplied dissemination data do not include microdata or sampling standard errors. The interval is descriptive, not a design-based survey interval.

Validation classification

Temporal holdouts use only earlier rows at each origin but rely on the latest published vintage unless explicitly stated. The business test is leave-one-country-out cross-sectional validation, not a future-year temporal backtest. Snapshot governance analysis is not presented as forecast validation. User scenarios are deterministic assumptions, not empirical forecasts.
