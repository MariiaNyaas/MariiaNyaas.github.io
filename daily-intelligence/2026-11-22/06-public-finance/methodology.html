The Sovereign Interest-Pass-Through Test

Research question

Can the policy rate improve Norway’s next-year net-interest baseline?

Data and calculation

SSB 10721, consolidated general government, nominal NOK million, interest income A51 and interest expense B41, 1995–2024. Norges Bank daily key policy rate aggregated to annual mean from 2000. Net position = income − expense. At each origin fit next-year net interest on current net interest, current rate level and rate change using only earlier rows; compare with persistence. Retain final ten origins. Circular two-year blocks, 5,000 draws.

Identification boundary

Nominal consolidated general-government interest flows are not the structural non-oil balance, fiscal-rule headroom, debt duration or sovereign solvency. Annual average policy rates are a coarse signal; asset composition, currency, maturity, GPFG income and accounting classifications are omitted. Latest-vintage, not real-time-vintage validation.

Validation classification

Temporal holdouts use only earlier rows at each origin but rely on the latest published vintage unless explicitly stated. The business test is leave-one-country-out cross-sectional validation, not a future-year temporal backtest. Snapshot governance analysis is not presented as forecast validation. User scenarios are deterministic assumptions, not empirical forecasts.
