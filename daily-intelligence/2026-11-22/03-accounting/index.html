The Working-Capital Warning Test

Receivables, inventory and payables as a forward cash-conversion diagnostic

EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE · EXPLICIT LIMITATIONS · USER SCENARIOS

Research Intelligence Brief

  1. The panel contains 5 SEC issuers and 15 issuer-year holdouts.
  2. The working-capital model records MAE 5.41 pp of CFO margin versus 4.87 pp for the prior-year margin.
  3. The candidate is worse by 0.54 pp on the point estimate.
  4. The issuer-cluster 95% interval for baseline-minus-model improvement is -1.89 to 0.81 pp and includes zero.

The mechanism and the test

Working-capital absorption can precede weaker cash conversion, but receivables and inventory may also finance real growth. A warning indicator is useful only if it improves the next filing beyond the prior cash-flow margin and remains stable across issuers.

Methodology & limitations

SEC Company Facts for Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, NVIDIA and Oracle, USD, form 10-K, filed no later than 21 November 2026. Annual flow duration 330–380 days. CFO margin = operating cash flow / revenue. Working-capital absorption = (Δreceivables + Δinventory − Δcurrent payables) / revenue. For the final three eligible targets per issuer, fit a pooled ridge model on earlier non-holdout issuer-years using lag CFO margin and lag absorption; compare with lag CFO margin. Resample issuers as clusters, 5,000 draws.

Six technology issuers are not a representative economy-wide panel. Company Facts tags, fiscal calendars and restatements differ. Working-capital absorption covers receivables, inventory and current payables only; it excludes deferred revenue, contract assets and other accruals. Latest-vintage SEC facts are not an archived real-time backtest.

What Would Change Our Mind

  • Footnote-reconciled working capital including contract balances and deferred revenue.
  • A pre-specified sector expansion with an untouched filing period.
  • A stable advantage under alternative tag fallbacks and restatement vintages.
