A fair baseline

The random walk is tested alongside a model containing only the currency’s own dynamics. Rate information must add value beyond that second benchmark.

Time-respecting evaluation

Only outcomes available at each forecast origin belong in the training set. Multi-month targets require an explicit gap before evaluation.

A test, not a trading claim

Forecast accuracy does not establish executable returns. The treasury tool uses user-supplied contract assumptions.

Which dimensions actually help?

Compare matched models with and without a feature block. Positive improvement means the larger model made smaller errors.

Feature-block comparison

Feature contribution is a predictive comparison. It is not evidence of a causal economic effect. Correlated inputs can share or substitute for one another’s information.

SEPARATE HISTORICAL DESIGN

What follows ECB announcement repricing?

One-year OIS repricing around ECB events is compared with later Norges Bank fixings. This is an event association, not a policy-rate shock or a trading forecast.

This module uses 1, 5 and 20 subsequent business-day fixings. Its event horizons are separate from the monthly forecasting model. The benchmark fixing is strictly before the event; the outcome fixing is strictly after it.
