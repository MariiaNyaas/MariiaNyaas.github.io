EUR/NOK Research Lab: methodology & evidence

Eleven input series, twelve full-model features, nine models and three forecast horizons. Complexity is evaluated against a common benchmark.

Decision supported by the evidence

Retain the zero-return random walk as the operational reference. The full model is a diagnostic and scenario instrument. Its expanded information set has not demonstrated a reliable forecast advantage. The fixed ensemble has a modest point advantage at longer horizons, with uncertainty that includes no advantage.

Question, target and sample

Does relative monetary policy, energy, risk and macroeconomic information improve future EUR/NOK forecasts, and does the rate block add information beyond the other blocks? The target is 100 × log(S[t+h] / S[t]), for horizons 1, 3 and 6 months. S is the last available Norges Bank daily fixing in each month, quoted as NOK per EUR; positive changes mean NOK depreciation.

The panel covers January 2000–October 2026. The complete common feature sample begins March 2006. Expanding evaluation starts January 2015 and contains 141, 139 and 136 matured forecasts. The original experiment used monthly-average exchange rates; its MAE cannot be compared directly with the new end-of-month MAE.

Prespecified model ladder

  1. Zero-return random walk.
  2. AR(1): previous monthly FX return and intercept.
  3. AR(1) plus Norwegian policy-rate level and change.
  4. AR(1) plus Norway–ECB deposit-rate gap, gap change and ECB MRO–deposit corridor.
  5. Relative-rate model plus lagged one- and three-month Brent changes.
  6. Previous model plus log VIX, VIX change, USD return and trailing six-month FX volatility.
  7. Full model: previous model plus inflation and industrial-production growth gaps.
  8. Full model plus rate-gap and oil interactions with lagged VIX ≥25.
  9. Fixed ensemble: 50% random walk, 25% AR(1), 25% full model.

Multivariate models use ridge alpha=10. Feature standardisation is fitted within each training window; the intercept is unpenalised. AR(1) uses OLS. Hyperparameters and ensemble weights were not selected on evaluation scores. Model implementation.

Validation, attribution and uncertainty

Models share forecast origins and complete training rows. Each direct-horizon regression uses only labels matured by its origin, with at least 60 observations; immature future targets are purged. MAE, RMSE and direction are reported. Direction accuracy alone does not establish profitability.

Paired MAE differences use 3,000 circular-block draws: length max(3,h), with sensitivity max(6,2h). Approximate centred-bootstrap p-values receive Holm adjustment across 45 model/benchmark/horizon comparisons. Marginal intervals remain descriptive. Separately refitted drop-one-block ablations isolate rates, energy, risk and macro on identical samples; their intervals and regime/subperiod results are exploratory.

Empirical bands use the latest 60 matured forecast errors, with at least 24 required. The full six-month model covered only 74/107 outcomes, or 69.16%, despite 5th/95th-percentile construction. These are not dependable 90% risk limits.

Separate historical ECB event study

EA-MPD one-year OIS repricing in the Monetary Event Window is paired with 292 events. Baseline: last Norges Bank fixing strictly before the event date. Outcomes: first, fifth and twentieth fixings strictly after it. A same-day 14:15 fixing is never presented as the complete ECB reaction.

Governance, reproduction and prospective test

The same-session independent code audit passed eight check groups, including source hashes, timing perturbations and reproduction after removing future records. This is not external peer review. Scenarios are conditional fitted associations; treasury outputs use explicit user assumptions rather than executable quotes.

The October snapshot was calculated on 24 November, not issued in October. First prospective month-end origin after the protocol freeze: 30 November 2026; save forecasts before outcomes mature. No automated monthly scheduler is configured.
