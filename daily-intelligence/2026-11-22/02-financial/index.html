THE BURDEN OF PROOF

What does the research support?

The decision tools use explicit exposures and stresses. They do not depend on a validated directional forecast.

No validated forecasting edge

Nine specifications were tested at 1, 3 and 6 months. No advantage survives the declared evidence checks. A point ranking is not a trading signal.

Research horizon

Absolute forecast error

MAE, percentage points of cumulative log EUR/NOK change. Lower is better.

Improvement versus random walk

Positive is better. Nominal 95% block-bootstrap intervals.

Fair comparisons

Random walk and own-history AR(1), matched forecast origins, expanding training windows and explicit label maturity.

Historical limitations

Latest-vintage macro data include revisions. October model outputs were calculated on 24 November, not issued live in October.

Decision consequence

This research does not justify a tactical hedge-policy override on its forecasting signal alone. Explicit stresses remain usable arithmetic.

The 6-month full model’s 5th–95th residual band covered 74/107 outcomes (69.2%). It is not a dependable 90% risk limit. Research data end October 2026; prospective origin begins 30 November 2026. Complete methodology.
