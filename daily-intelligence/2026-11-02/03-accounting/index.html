Reproducibility

Calculation method

Illustrative readiness score = Σ(weight_i × status_i), where Ready=1, Partial=0.5, Missing=0. Weights sum to 100 and are analyst assumptions: scope 15, boundary 15, materiality 10, IRO linkage 15, lineage 15, owners 10, cross-references 10, assurance trail 10.

Illustrative worked example: the default synthetic evidence statuses produce a weighted readiness score of 30/100. This is not a firm assessment, regulatory score or assurance conclusion.

Management implication

Decision lens

Prioritise boundary, data lineage and cross-report connectivity before drafting narrative disclosures. Obtain legal advice on scope and do not treat an exposure draft as adopted law.
