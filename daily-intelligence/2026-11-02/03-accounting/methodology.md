# Methodology — ESRS-40a: From Reporting Scope to Audit-Ready Evidence

**Edition:** 2026-11-02  \n**Status:** Independent analysis, primary-source snapshot.

## Research question

Which evidence-control gaps should a cross-border reporting team remediate before assurance planning?

## Official observations and trigger

EFRAG held its final ESRS-40a outreach event on 29 October 2026, ahead of the 31 October consultation deadline for certain non-EU undertakings.


## Reproducible model

Illustrative readiness score = Σ(weight_i × status_i), where Ready=1, Partial=0.5, Missing=0. Weights sum to 100 and are analyst assumptions: scope 15, boundary 15, materiality 10, IRO linkage 15, lineage 15, owners 10, cross-references 10, assurance trail 10.

**Worked example:** Illustrative worked example: the default synthetic evidence statuses produce a weighted readiness score of 30/100. This is not a firm assessment, regulatory score or assurance conclusion.

All user controls are hypothetical analyst-defined inputs. Recalculate in browser by changing the controls; the model is contained in the self-contained `index.html`. The data snapshot is in `data.json`.

## Limitations and validation boundaries

The eight checks and their weights are an original illustrative governance diagnostic, not a list of ESRS-40a mandatory requirements or a published assurance standard. All starting statuses are synthetic. ESRS-40a was a consultation exposure draft at the cited event; no company-specific compliance conclusion is made.

## Management decision

Prioritise boundary, data lineage and cross-report connectivity before drafting narrative disclosures. Obtain legal advice on scope and do not treat an exposure draft as adopted law.
