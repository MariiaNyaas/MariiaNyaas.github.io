## ESRS-40a: From Reporting Scope to Audit-Ready Evidence

**02 November 2026 | Independent institutional research**

**The decision question.** Which evidence-control gaps should a cross-border reporting team remediate before assurance planning?

**Executive assessment.** EFRAG’s 29 October outreach placed the ESRS-40a consultation at the intersection of comparability, feasibility and assurance. For non-EU groups with European activity, the immediate management question is evidence readiness, not the production of a polished sustainability narrative. This case supplies a transparent eight-control diagnostic covering scoping, boundaries, materiality, connectivity, data lineage and assurance trails. The weighted score is intentionally synthetic and should be replaced by a documented control matrix before use in a real engagement. It is not a compliance opinion. The recommended next step is to establish an accountable evidence owner for each material disclosure and to reconcile the reporting perimeter with financial consolidation and legal scope.

**Worked example.** Illustrative worked example: the default synthetic evidence statuses produce a weighted readiness score of 30/100. This is not a firm assessment, regulatory score or assurance conclusion.

**Recommended action.** Prioritise boundary, data lineage and cross-report connectivity before drafting narrative disclosures. Obtain legal advice on scope and do not treat an exposure draft as adopted law.

**Evidence constraints.** The eight checks and their weights are an original illustrative governance diagnostic, not a list of ESRS-40a mandatory requirements or a published assurance standard. All starting statuses are synthetic. ESRS-40a was a consultation exposure draft at the cited event; no company-specific compliance conclusion is made.
