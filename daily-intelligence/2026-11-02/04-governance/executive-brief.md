# Executive Brief | Risk & Governance Intelligence

## Tokenised Collateral: Speed Is Not the Same as Liquidity

**02 November 2026 | Independent institutional research**

**The decision question.** What usable collateral remains after market haircuts, legal holdbacks and delayed mobilisation?

**Executive assessment.** Finanstilsynet’s 26 October notice brings ESMA’s tokenised-collateral consultation into the Norwegian market context. The central risk-management issue is not whether distributed-ledger technology accelerates settlement in normal conditions, but whether the collateral remains legally enforceable, segregated, transferable and liquid under stress. The interactive waterfall separates market valuation, operational/legal restrictions and mobilisation delay. None of its stress parameters is an observed market statistic or regulatory haircut. It is a board-level discussion tool: a CCP or clearing member should seek asset-specific evidence and contractual analysis before assigning liquidity value to tokenised collateral.

**Worked example.** Illustrative worked example: €100m collateral with a 6% market haircut, 8% operational/legal holdback and 1.5% daily adverse move for two days produces approximately €83.91m usable value, or a €6.09m shortfall against a hypothetical €90m requirement.

**Recommended action.** Require legal finality, custody segregation, operational fallback and liquidity testing before treating tokenised collateral as equivalent to immediately available traditional collateral.

**Evidence constraints.** ESMA is gathering evidence; the consultation does not establish new collateral eligibility or haircut rules. Every monetary value, haircut, delay and adverse price move in this dashboard is illustrative and not calibrated to any CCP or tokenised asset.
