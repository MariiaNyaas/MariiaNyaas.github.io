Historical forecast validation

Target: Aggregate retail investment (seasonally adjusted). 87 quarterly observations. Expanding-window, one-step-ahead out-of-sample predictions for 39 quarters (2017Q1–2026Q3); the model is re-estimated at every forecast origin using only earlier target observations. The chart displays the last 12 holdout forecasts. The benchmark is the previous quarter's observation, except for unadjusted series where the same quarter of the prior year is used.

Illustrative adjusted forecast: 98.0. This is a deterministic sensitivity exercise, not a calibrated shock-response estimate.

Predictive uncertainty and diagnostic interpretation

Prequential absolute-error interval diagnostic: nominal 90th-percentile residual radius, recalibrated using only prior test errors after twelve holdout observations. Observed coverage: 81.5% across 27 eligible forecasts. This is an empirical diagnostic, not a guaranteed 90% prediction interval.

Evidence boundary: Model underperforms naive. Investment is not tax revenue; no fiscal elasticity or revenue forecast is claimed. All source series are latest-vintage revised statistics, not real-time vintage snapshots; future revisions may alter historical estimates. The tested architecture failed to outperform its benchmark on RMSE.

Methodology and reproducibility

Data source: Statistics Norway, table 07266. Forecasts use the index-level values from the downloaded spreadsheet, without cross-series contemporaneous inputs. The first 48 observations are the minimum training window; 39 later observations form an expanding-origin evaluation. Model specification: AR(1) on four-quarter growth. Comparison metric: RMSE in index points. No causal inference, no tax revenue or default-rate estimation. Historical forecast scores do not establish future performance.
