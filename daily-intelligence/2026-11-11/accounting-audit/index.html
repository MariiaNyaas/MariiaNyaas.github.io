Predictive uncertainty and diagnostic interpretation

Prequential absolute-error interval diagnostic: nominal 90th-percentile residual radius, recalibrated using only prior test errors after twelve holdout observations. Observed coverage: 88.9% across 27 eligible forecasts. This is an empirical diagnostic, not a guaranteed 90% prediction interval.

Evidence boundary: A sector investment forecasting diagnostic, not an audit opinion or proof of misstatement. All source series are latest-vintage revised statistics, not real-time vintage snapshots; future revisions may alter historical estimates. The architecture outperformed the specified naive benchmark on this historical window, but the advantage is not established as statistically significant.

Methodology and reproducibility

Data source: Statistics Norway, table 07266. Forecasts use the index-level values from the downloaded spreadsheet, without cross-series contemporaneous inputs. The first 48 observations are the minimum training window; 39 later observations form an expanding-origin evaluation. Model specification: Seasonal AR(1,4). Comparison metric: RMSE in index points. No causal inference, no tax revenue or default-rate estimation. Historical forecast scores do not establish future performance.
