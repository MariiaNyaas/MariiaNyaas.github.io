Historical forecast validation

Predictive uncertainty and diagnostic interpretation

Prequential absolute-error interval diagnostic: nominal 90th-percentile residual radius, recalibrated using only prior test errors after twelve holdout observations. Observed coverage: 81.5% across 27 eligible forecasts. This is an empirical diagnostic, not a guaranteed 90% prediction interval.

Evidence boundary: Model underperforms seasonal naive. Negative finding is retained; not a superior forecasting tool. All source series are latest-vintage revised statistics, not real-time vintage snapshots; future revisions may alter historical estimates. The tested architecture failed to outperform its benchmark on RMSE.

Methodology and reproducibility

Data source: Statistics Norway, table 07266. Forecasts use the index-level values from the downloaded spreadsheet, without cross-series contemporaneous inputs. The first 48 observations are the minimum training window; 39 later observations form an expanding-origin evaluation. Model specification: Seasonal differenced drift. Comparison metric: RMSE in index points. No causal inference, no tax revenue or default-rate estimation. Historical forecast scores do not establish future performance.
