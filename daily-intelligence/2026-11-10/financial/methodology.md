# Methodology — The Energy Weighting Trap in Norwegian Nominal Growth

Source: https://www.ssb.no/en/priser-og-prisindekser/produsent-og-engrosprisindekser/statistikk/produsentprisindeksen

Observed release figures:
- Total PPI Oct 2026 m/m: 6.7
- Energy PPI Oct 2026 m/m: 10
- Non-energy PPI Oct 2026 m/m: 0.6
- Energy PPI weight per 1000: 496

The interactive scenario computes w*A+(1-w)*B. This is not an official weighted index and should not be interpreted if observations are in incompatible units. No time-series regression, backtest or causal validation was performed. Independent longitudinal evidence, vintage data, alternative hypotheses and uncertainty estimates are outstanding.
