# Research Intelligence Brief — The Energy Weighting Trap in Norwegian Nominal Growth

**Thesis:** Energy-price acceleration may exaggerate the breadth of nominal producer-price growth.

**Primary observations:**
- Total PPI Oct 2026 m/m: 6.7
- Energy PPI Oct 2026 m/m: 10
- Non-energy PPI Oct 2026 m/m: 0.6
- Energy PPI weight per 1000: 496

**Decision:** Decompose nominal exposures by energy intensity before changing growth forecasts.

**Alternative:** Price mix, sector weights, revisions or temporary shocks could explain the signal.

**Falsification:** Compare independent firm-level exposure and historical official revisions.

**Status:** Exploratory, unvalidated, not investment advice.
