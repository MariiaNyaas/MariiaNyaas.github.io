Investor / Decision Matrix

Conditional signal

Falling wage-agreement coverage makes extrapolation of negotiated wage trackers increasingly conditional.

Decision

Reconcile contracted payroll obligations with tracker coverage, one-off components and actual headcount.

Competing explanation

Price mix, changing weights, temporary shocks, measurement revisions and exposure heterogeneity can produce similar aggregate patterns.

What would change our mind?

Firm-level exposure, official vintages or later observations inconsistent with the proposed mechanism.

Interactive diagnostic — illustrative, not a forecast

Only like-for-like numerical observations may be meaningfully blended. Cross-series units, aggregation weights and periods may differ; this control demonstrates sensitivity, not an official index or causal model.

Methodology and limitations

Two-point weighted sensitivity: w×A+(1−w)×B. The weight is hypothetical and does not reproduce official aggregation. A four-point release snapshot cannot support a time-series backtest, causal identification, statistical prediction intervals or quantified predictive accuracy. Independent longitudinal data and real-time vintage archives are required before production use.
