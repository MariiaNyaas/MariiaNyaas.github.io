# Research Intelligence Brief

**Thesis:** Retail volume changes alone cannot identify nominal VAT revenue without prices, tax mix and compliance.

- EU retail Sep m/m %: -0.6
- EU retail Sep y/y %: 0.9
- Euro area retail Sep y/y %: 0.4
- Euro area non-food Sep m/m %: -1.2

**Decision:** Reconcile nominal taxable consumption, exemptions, rate mix and payment lags.

**Alternative explanations:** Seasonality, changing weights, revisions and temporary sector shocks.

**What would change our mind:** Independent evidence inconsistent with the mechanism.

**Research status:** Exploratory and not validated as a forecast. Not investment advice.
