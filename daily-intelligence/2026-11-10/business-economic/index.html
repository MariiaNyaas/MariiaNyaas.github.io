Investor / Decision Matrix

Decision implication

Monitor inventory days, discounting, category mix and reported gross margins.

Proposed mechanism

A disproportionate decline in non-food retail volumes may be an early signal of markdown pressure, but firm margins must be tested separately.

Alternative explanation

Seasonality, category weights, price changes, measurement revisions or temporary sector shocks can mimic the pattern.

What would change our mind?

New official vintages, independent firm-level evidence or changes in the underlying denominator that contradict the hypothesis.
