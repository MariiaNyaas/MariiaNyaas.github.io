# Research Intelligence Brief

**Thesis:** A disproportionate decline in non-food retail volumes may be an early signal of markdown pressure, but firm margins must be tested separately.

- Euro area retail Sep m/m %: -0.7
- Non-food retail Sep m/m %: -1.2
- Food retail Sep m/m %: -0.3
- Fuel retail Sep m/m %: 1

**Decision:** Monitor inventory days, discounting, category mix and reported gross margins.

**Alternative explanations:** Seasonality, changing weights, revisions and temporary sector shocks.

**What would change our mind:** Independent evidence inconsistent with the mechanism.

**Research status:** Exploratory and not validated as a forecast. Not investment advice.
