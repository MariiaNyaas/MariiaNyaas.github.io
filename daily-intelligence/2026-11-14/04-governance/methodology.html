The Stewardship-Cell Dominance Map

Research question

Single-name dependence inside country–industry review cells

Data and calculation

Read the NBIM 30 June 2026 equity snapshot in UTF-16, semicolon separated. Retain rows with numeric market value, country and industry. Group by reported country × reported industry. Within each cell calculate total value, number of holdings, top issuer share, HHI and effective number of names. Default eligibility requires at least three holdings; dominance requires top issuer share at least 50%. Denominator for value share is all exported listed-equity value. Sensitivity thresholds: 30/40/50/60/70%. Snapshot analysis; forecast backtesting is not applicable.

Identification boundary

One 30 June 2026 listed-equity snapshot. A country-industry cell is an analytical grouping, not a legal or voting-control perimeter. Market value is not risk contribution. No return history means backtesting and causal claims are not applicable.

Validation classification

Where a forward test is shown, it is explicitly latest-vintage and uses only earlier rows at each origin. A snapshot or definitional analysis is not presented as forecast validation. User scenarios are deterministic assumption laboratories and are not empirical forecasts.
