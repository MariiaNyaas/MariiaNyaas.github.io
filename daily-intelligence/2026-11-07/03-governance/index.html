Headline Versus Underlying Inflation: Policy Signal Risk

When does headline inflation diverge enough from underlying inflation to mislead risk monitoring?

Research status: exploratory empirical study. Historical descriptive estimates and scenario sensitivities are not causal forecasts. Data coverage, comparability and model limitations are disclosed below.

Research intelligence brief

Across 2004–2025, headline and underlying annual inflation co-move (exploratory OLS R²≈0.59). Their difference nevertheless varies substantially, reaching approximately −1.74 percentage points in 2020 and +1.85 points in 2022.

Methodology, source and limitations

Source: Statistics Norway, tables 14708. Annual inflation rates derived from averages of monthly seasonally adjusted CPI and CPI-ATE indices. The gap is not a causal energy effect.

Calculations use historical observations through 2025, except the initial 2026 public R&D budget which is explicitly a budget allocation rather than a realised outcome. Recalculated Pearson correlations use pairwise observations in the chosen time window. Rebased values divide each selected series by its first value and multiply by 100; zero baseline is excluded. Scenario results are deterministic and do not incorporate confidence intervals, dynamic feedback, changing composition, omitted factors or out-of-sample validation.
