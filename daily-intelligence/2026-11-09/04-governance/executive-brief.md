# Research Intelligence Brief — The Monetary-Policy Decision Gap: How to Govern a No-Change Rate

**Thesis:** An unchanged policy rate can coexist with changing macro risks; board triggers should depend on risk limits, not the rate headline.

**Source-verified indicators:**
- Policy rate: 4.5%
- CPI: 3.4%
- CPI-ATE: 3.0%
- Target: 2.0%

**Decision matrix**

| Signal | Interpretation | Confirmatory evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Source indicator changes | Possible change in risk transmission | Match source definitions and revision history |
| Scenario sensitivity rises | Conditional exposure under stated assumptions | Obtain actual contractual / operational exposure |
| Competing explanation dominates | Initial thesis may not hold | Backtesting of firm-level risk triggers shows no material incremental value beyond existing risk limits. |

**Action:** Set documented board escalation thresholds and test false positives against historical outcomes.

**Uncertainty:** The policy decision includes FX, labour market and energy shocks; a single inflation reading is not sufficient.
