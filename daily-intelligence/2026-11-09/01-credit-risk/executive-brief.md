# Research Intelligence Brief — The Rate-Hold Credit Cliff: Refinancing, Cash Flow and Covenant Headroom

**Thesis:** A policy-rate hold is not a neutral credit event: refinancing spreads and debt maturities can still erode covenant headroom.

**Source-verified indicators:**
- Policy rate: 4.50%
- Sep CPI: 3.4%
- Sep CPI-ATE: 3.0%
- Inflation target: 2.0%

**Decision matrix**

| Signal | Interpretation | Confirmatory evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Source indicator changes | Possible change in risk transmission | Match source definitions and revision history |
| Scenario sensitivity rises | Conditional exposure under stated assumptions | Obtain actual contractual / operational exposure |
| Competing explanation dominates | Initial thesis may not hold | Company disclosures show fixed-rate protection, earlier refinancing or offsetting EBITDA improvement. |

**Action:** Review maturity ladders and covenants before assuming unchanged policy rates mean unchanged credit risk.

**Uncertainty:** Covenant buffers depend on hedges, amortisation, cash holdings, lender margins and EBITDA sensitivity.
