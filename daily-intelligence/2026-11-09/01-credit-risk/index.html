Economic mechanism

A policy-rate hold is not a neutral credit event: refinancing spreads and debt maturities can still erode covenant headroom.

Competing explanation

Covenant buffers depend on hedges, amortisation, cash holdings, lender margins and EBITDA sensitivity.

Falsification test

Company disclosures show fixed-rate protection, earlier refinancing or offsetting EBITDA improvement.

Decision implication

Review maturity ladders and covenants before assuming unchanged policy rates mean unchanged credit risk.

Interactive sensitivity · Not empirical estimation

Change the assumptions. Inspect the consequence.
