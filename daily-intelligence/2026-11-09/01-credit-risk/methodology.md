# Methodology: The Rate-Hold Credit Cliff: Refinancing, Cash Flow and Covenant Headroom

Publication: 2026-11-09
Primary release: Norges Bank, 5 Nov 2026 — https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/news/Press-releases/2026/2026-11-05-rate/

## Observed facts
- Policy rate: 4.50%
- Sep CPI: 3.4%
- Sep CPI-ATE: 3.0%
- Inflation target: 2.0%

## Hypothesis
A policy-rate hold is not a neutral credit event: refinancing spreads and debt maturities can still erode covenant headroom.

## Deterministic model
newICR = baselineICR / (1 + floatingShare × spreadShock / 10000 / assumedBaselineInterestRate)

## Inputs
- Floating debt share: 65% (range 0–100)
- Refinancing spread shock: 150bp (range 0–500)
- Interest cover baseline: 3.2× (range 1–8)

## Interpretation and limitations
Illustrative 6.5% starting borrowing cost, fixed EBITDA and principal. This is not a portfolio PD model.

## Competing explanation
Covenant buffers depend on hedges, amortisation, cash holdings, lender margins and EBITDA sensitivity.

## Falsification
Company disclosures show fixed-rate protection, earlier refinancing or offsetting EBITDA improvement.

## Research status
Exploratory scenario, not causal identification, calibrated forecast, portfolio recommendation or empirical backtest. Observed indicators must not be treated as the model's calibrated parameters.
