# Methodology

## Source
https://www.ssb.no/energi-og-industri/industri-og-bergverksdrift/statistikk/produksjonsindeks-for-olje-og-gass-industri-bergverk-og-kraftforsyning/artikler/nesten-uendra-industriproduksjon-i-3.kvartal

## Hypothesis
An almost flat manufacturing aggregate conceals opposing sector movements; investment implications require exposure weights, not headline inference.

## Observations
Manufacturing Q3 2026: 0.1
Shipbuilding: 2.4
Chemicals: -3.8
Metals: -2.1

## Scenario formula
(weights[0]*2.4-weights[1]*3.8-weights[2]*2.1)/100

## Limitations
The selected weights are user assumptions, not SSB's official industry weights; unmodelled industries are assumed flat solely for illustration.

## Falsification
Test official industry weights and subsequent production data; a broad reversal would weaken the rotation hypothesis.

Not a calibrated predictive model.
