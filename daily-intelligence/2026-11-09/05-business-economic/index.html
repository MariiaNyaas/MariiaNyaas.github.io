Economic mechanism

A flat aggregate can hide opposite sector movements; investable implications depend on economic weights and persistence, not raw percentage changes.

Competing explanation

Different sector output shares, seasonal adjustment, index aggregation and petroleum supplier demand can explain aggregate stability.

Falsification test

Official weighted contribution series show negligible sector concentration or subsequent months reverse the divergence.

Decision implication

Inspect sector-weighted earnings exposures and avoid inferring business-cycle stability from the +0.1% headline.
