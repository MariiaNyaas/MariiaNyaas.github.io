# Research Intelligence Brief

An almost flat manufacturing aggregate conceals opposing sector movements; investment implications require exposure weights, not headline inference.

- Manufacturing Q3 2026: 0.1
- Shipbuilding: 2.4
- Chemicals: -3.8
- Metals: -2.1

## Decision matrix

| Scenario | Implication | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Conditional sensitivity | Reconcile assumptions to exposures |
| Adverse | Re-evaluate buffer or capital | Independent source and accounting verification |
| Thesis falsified | Withdraw inference | Test official industry weights and subsequent production data; a broad reversal would weaken the rotation hypothesis. |
