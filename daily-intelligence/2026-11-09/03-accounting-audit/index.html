Economic mechanism

Wage agreements can create a timing and recognition problem: headline negotiated growth and permanent recurring pay need not match.

Competing explanation

Coverage of the ECB tracker varies materially by horizon; one-off timing, headcount and social costs affect actual accruals.

Falsification test

Payroll records, contracts and accrual schedules reconcile recurring and one-off payments without unexplained variances.

Decision implication

Separate permanent pay increases from one-offs in audit sampling, forecasting and accrual testing.

Interactive sensitivity · Not empirical estimation

Change the assumptions. Inspect the consequence.
