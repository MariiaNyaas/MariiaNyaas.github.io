# Research Intelligence Brief — The Wage-Commitment Audit: Why One-Off Pay Distorts 2027 Cost Estimates

**Thesis:** Wage agreements can create a timing and recognition problem: headline negotiated growth and permanent recurring pay need not match.

**Source-verified indicators:**
- H2 2026 headline: 2.7%
- H1 2027 headline: 2.8%
- Q3 2027 headline: 2.4%
- H1 2027 coverage: 31.8%

**Decision matrix**

| Signal | Interpretation | Confirmatory evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Source indicator changes | Possible change in risk transmission | Match source definitions and revision history |
| Scenario sensitivity rises | Conditional exposure under stated assumptions | Obtain actual contractual / operational exposure |
| Competing explanation dominates | Initial thesis may not hold | Payroll records, contracts and accrual schedules reconcile recurring and one-off payments without unexplained variances. |

**Action:** Separate permanent pay increases from one-offs in audit sampling, forecasting and accrual testing.

**Uncertainty:** Coverage of the ECB tracker varies materially by horizon; one-off timing, headcount and social costs affect actual accruals.
