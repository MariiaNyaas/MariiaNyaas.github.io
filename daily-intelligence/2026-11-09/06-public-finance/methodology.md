# Methodology

## Source
https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2026/html/ecb.blog20261104~78299c3eaf.en.html

## Hypothesis
Fiscal resilience can be understated when public capital appraisal excludes contingent climate losses and avoided-loss benefits.

## Observations
Publication date: 4 Nov 2026
Hazard focus: Heat, drought, wildfire
Geography: Europe
Research status: Scenario, not calibration

## Scenario formula
(weights[0]/100)*weights[1]*(1-weights[2]/100)

## Limitations
The ECB article supplies the hazard narrative, not a calibrated probability, severity or mitigation effect. All slider values are illustrative.

## Falsification
Reconcile exposure maps, insurance claims, local loss histories and mitigation costs; observed losses may contradict assumed distributions.

Not a calibrated predictive model.
