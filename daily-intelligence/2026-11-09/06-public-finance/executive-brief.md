# Research Intelligence Brief

Fiscal resilience can be understated when public capital appraisal excludes contingent climate losses and avoided-loss benefits.

- Publication date: 4 Nov 2026
- Hazard focus: Heat, drought, wildfire
- Geography: Europe
- Research status: Scenario, not calibration

## Decision matrix

| Scenario | Implication | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Conditional sensitivity | Reconcile assumptions to exposures |
| Adverse | Re-evaluate buffer or capital | Independent source and accounting verification |
| Thesis falsified | Withdraw inference | Reconcile exposure maps, insurance claims, local loss histories and mitigation costs; observed losses may contradict assumed distributions. |
