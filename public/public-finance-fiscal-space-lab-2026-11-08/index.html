Methodology, source and audit controls

Source reference: https://www.ssb.no/en/statbank1/. The supplied SSB datasets do not include municipal tax revenue, spending, debt stocks or interest rates. All defaults are hypothetical, not published fiscal aggregates.

Validation status: deterministic scenario calculator, not an econometrically fitted or backtested model. Scenario sensitivities are conditional arithmetic, not estimated causal effects or forecasts. No source revision, confidence interval or probability distribution is implied by the slider positions.

Reproduction: the exact defaults, ranges and calculation equations are embedded in the page source. Change any variable and recompute; reset restores the published reference scenario. For institution-specific application, substitute independently verified inputs and document assumptions, units, controls and data vintages.
